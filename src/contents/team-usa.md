# Motivation

As a big Olympics fan, I wanted to explore the publicly available roster data for Team USA. The dataset covers athletes from the 2016 Rio Summer Olympics and the 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics, including their sport, event, and age at the time of competition.

# Visualization design

The core view is a histogram of Team USA athletes distributed by age, broken down by sport. The five Olympic ring colors are used to encode sports categories, keeping the visual language tied to the Games. Sort and highlight controls let you drill into the data: sorting sports by median age, total athlete count, or alphabetically, and highlighting individual athletes or age groups to trace patterns across the roster.

# Insights

The age distribution varies dramatically by sport. This makes it easy to spot which sports skew young — like gymnastics — versus those where veterans compete well into their 30s and beyond. Gymnasts are among the youngest athletes on the roster, often competing in their teens. Equestrians sit at the opposite end of the spectrum — it's one of the few Olympic sports where athletes routinely compete in their 40s and 50s. Sports like shooting and sailing also tend to attract older, more experienced competitors, while swimming and track events show a broader spread centered in the mid-20s.
